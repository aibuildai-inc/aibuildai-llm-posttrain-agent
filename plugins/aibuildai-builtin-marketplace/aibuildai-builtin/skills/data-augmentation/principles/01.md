# Principle 1: Horizontal Flip

## Invariance it assumes

f(HFlip(x)) = f(x). Predictions are unchanged when left and right are swapped. For spatial labels (segmentation, detection, keypoints) the label is mirrored in lock-step: masks flip, bounding boxes have their x-coordinate mirrored, keypoints swap left/right limb indices.

## Mechanism

Mirror along the vertical axis: output[r, c] = input[r, W-1-c]. Deterministic, no interpolation. Inverse is itself.

## When this invariance typically holds

- Natural-scene classification where class does not depend on horizontal orientation (cats, dogs, cars from generic angles).
- Semantic segmentation with category-only labels (road, vegetation, building) lacking directional semantics.
- Horizontally-uniform natural textures: grass, water, sand, sky, soil.
- 2D cross-sections of materials with no preferred lateral direction: metallographic slides, isotropic composites.
- Fundus and dermoscopy lesion classification when no laterality label is attached.
- Overhead imagery with no compass-direction label.

## When this invariance breaks

- **Left/right-labelled classes**: left-turn vs right-turn road signs; left-lung vs right-lung disease localization; left-hand vs right-hand pose; laterality-coded medical findings.
- **Script-containing images** where the task reads or depends on text: document OCR, scene-text detection/recognition, handwriting, any task with embedded letters or digits.
- **Driver-view / dash-cam in a fixed-hand-drive country**: steering wheel, driver's hand position, road-sign side all flip under HFlip.
- **Tasks depending on directional features**: "vehicle facing right" classification, "animal walking left" detection.
- **Paired / stereo inputs** where left and right camera feeds have fixed roles.

## Silent failure mode

HFlip never raises an error; loss curves look normal; only ceiling accuracy drops. Common symptom: training loss continues to decline but test accuracy plateaus below the non-augmented baseline. Detection requires ablation or per-class error inspection on a label known to correlate with directionality.

## TTA use

Safe iff training-time HFlip is safe AND the inverse on the output (mask mirror, box x-coord mirror) is implemented. Two-view HFlip TTA (identity + flip) is the first TTA added on safe tasks and usually contributes +0.001 to +0.01 at no training cost.

## Common misconception

"HFlip is universally safe." HFlip is safe on the majority of ImageNet-like tasks because those tasks happen to lack left/right label semantics, not because HFlip is mathematically invariant. Any task that depends on lateralized features — driving, radiology with laterality, text — breaks the invariance.
