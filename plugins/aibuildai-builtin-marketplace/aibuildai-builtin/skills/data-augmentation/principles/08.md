# Principle 8: Shear

## Invariance it assumes

f(Shear_{dx}(x)) = f(x) or f(Shear_{dy}(x)) = f(x) for small shear coefficients. The task is approximately invariant to non-orthogonal linear distortion of one axis with respect to the other.

## Mechanism

Apply an affine transform with non-zero off-diagonal elements: [[1, dx], [dy, 1]]. Typically only one direction is used at a time (horizontal shear dx, or vertical shear dy), with |dx|, |dy| ≤ 0.1. Implemented via `cv2.warpPerspective` or affine with a non-identity 2×2 linear part.

## When this invariance typically holds

- **Natural-scene images with viewing-angle variance at acquisition**.
- **Document images with slight page-tilt during scanning**.
- **Geological cross-sections where layers are not strictly horizontal** — some lateral deformation is physically realistic.
- **Handwritten-character recognition**: slant is a natural writer-to-writer variation and shear approximates it.
- **Some medical imaging** where slight patient rotation within the detector plane produces shear-like apparent distortion.

## When this invariance breaks

- **Rigid calibrated imaging**: quantitative MRI, CT with pixel calibration, any measurement imaging.
- **Tasks depending on strict axis-alignment**: grid-aligned structures, architectural floor plans, engineering drawings, QR codes / barcodes.
- **Label-orthogonal features**: tasks where the label depends on whether two features are perpendicular or parallel (e.g., cell-division angle relative to microtubule axis).
- **Fine thin-structure tasks**: shear can introduce interpolation artefacts that break thin connectivity.

## Relative to rotation

Shear is a close cousin of rotation — both are small linear distortions — but shear does **not** preserve right angles. On domains where right angles carry information (architecture, man-made structures, grid-aligned content), shear is more harmful than a rotation of comparable magnitude. Conversely, on flow-like textures (hair, fabric, soft tissue), shear is roughly interchangeable with a small rotation.

## Parameter range

Practical Kaggle solutions that use shear bound it to |dx| ≤ 0.07 or so. Above ±0.1 the distortion becomes visible and usually harmful in most domains. It is commonly placed in a `OneOf` bucket with rotation and crop-pad, so only one geometric warp is applied per sample.

## Common misconception

"Shear is just another rotation." Shear is not a rotation. A small horizontal shear shifts the top of the image relative to the bottom without rotating the overall image, creating a parallelogram. Where rotation preserves angles, shear does not — and on angle-sensitive tasks this is a real distinction.
