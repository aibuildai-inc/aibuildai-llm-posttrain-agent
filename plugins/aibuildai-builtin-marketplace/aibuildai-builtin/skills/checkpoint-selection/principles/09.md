# Principle 09: Fold-consistent selection rule

**Layer**: Cross-fold consistency. Operates on the rule itself, not on weights or predictions.

**Source**: Standard CV-evaluation methodology; Kaggle competition post-mortems on per-fold customization being a public-LB overfitting trap.

## Definition

Every fold of one design MUST use IDENTICAL checkpoint-selection rules. If fold 0 deploys SWA, every other fold MUST also deploy SWA. Per-fold customization (`fold_0 → epoch 7; fold_1 → epoch 14; fold_2 → public-LB-best`) is forbidden. The rule is a property of the design's training recipe, declared in the design plan; it is not a free variable for each fold's run.

## Mechanism

K-fold CV reports a mean across folds, which estimates the population generalization of the chosen *recipe*. When the rule differs per fold, the mean conflates K different estimators and is no longer interpretable as the generalization of any one rule. Worse, the per-fold customization is typically driven by inspecting each fold's val curve and picking the point that looks best — this is direct overfitting to val data, not selection over a held-out set. The reported CV is then optimistically biased by the same `√(2 log T)` factor as raw cherry-picking, but invisible to outside reviewers because the rule looks principled fold-by-fold.

## Pros

- The K-fold mean stays interpretable: the strategy itself is what is being evaluated, not lucky points.
- Public-LB / private-LB drift is reduced — a recipe that wins on CV is more likely to win on the test set when CV is not contaminated by per-fold overfitting.
- Reviewers can replay the rule from the plan; reproducibility is preserved.

## Cons

- Gives up the "rescue" of folds that look bad — you must accept the rule's verdict, even when one fold underperforms compared to a manually picked checkpoint.
- Requires committing to the rule before training (the rule is in the plan), not after seeing the curves.

## When to Use

Always. This is a hygiene rule, not an optional optimization. Every design's plan must name a single checkpoint-selection rule (one of Principles 01–08) that the Coder applies identically to every fold. The rule is captured in the design's `data_manifest_fold{k}.json:fold_split_protocol` field for downstream auditing.

## Common Misconception

That "I'm only choosing the best epoch per fold, that's standard". It is standard only when the rule (e.g., "best val_loss") is the SAME for every fold. Manual per-fold inspection — "this fold's curve looks weird so I'll pick epoch 9 instead of best" — breaks the rule and turns CV into a val-set fit.

## Load-Bearing Quote

> "When the rule for choosing the deployed model differs across folds, the K-fold mean estimates nothing in particular. Reviewers cannot replay your rule and your CV cannot be trusted as an estimate of generalization."
>
> — Standard cross-validation methodology, summarized in Kaggle CV-best-practice threads
